One circuit, two builds

Both games use the same assets: the Red Bull RB19 model and the road mesh from NFS Undercover DS — Highway Battle. They are otherwise completely separate builds by two different AI systems.

Built by Claude

APEX

The same road mesh, rebuilt as a real racing circuit — the two carriageways stitched together into a 3.87 km closed lap with a proper racing line, sector timing and AI opponents.

  • Five graphics tiers, topping out at APEX: temporal super-resolution, screen-space reflections, four-cascade contact shadows
  • Custom tyre model — Pacejka slip curves, not a single friction number
  • Race, time trial and practice, with lap and sector timing
Play APEX Built by Codex / ChatGPT

Highway Drive 1.0

The original build: free driving along the highway with weather, time-of-day and a parked ray-traced photo mode. Preserved exactly as it was, untouched.

  • Four quality presets, up to Ultra
  • Clear / rain / snow / fog and day / sunset / night
  • Genuine path-traced Photo Mode while parked
Play Highway Drive
