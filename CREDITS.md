# F1 Highway Drive — Preview

This is the **playable preview build** for GitHub Pages.

## Assets

- **Oracle Red Bull F1 Car RB19 2023** by Redgrund — CC BY 4.0. The preview uses the prepared compact GLB derived from the supplied RB19 asset.
- **NFS Undercover DS – Highway Battle** by amogusstrikesback2 — CC BY 4.0. The preview roadway geometry was extracted from the supplied highway GLB.

## Preview scope

The preview includes the supplied highway road geometry and collision barriers, the RB19 model with independently animated/steering wheels, acceleration, braking and reverse, gravity, grip/downforce tuning, camera presets, weather and time controls, touch controls, respawn, desktop controls and quality presets.

This remains a preview. The final pass will expand the vehicle simulation, graphics, weather, camera behaviour and asset fidelity.
